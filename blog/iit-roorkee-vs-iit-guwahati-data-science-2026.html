IIT Roorkee vs IIT Guwahati Data Science Certificate 2026 — Which Is Better?

August 05, 2026 · 7 min read · datasciencecourse.in

IIT Roorkee vs IIT Guwahati DS — The Quick Answer

Both are 11-month IIT data science certificates at similar fee points with IIT alumni status. IIT Roorkee (₹2.5L, NIRF #7 Engineering) has a stronger brand nationally — particularly in North India and with large tech employers. IIT Guwahati (₹2.1L, NIRF #8 Engineering, EICT delivery) is more affordable with a comparable curriculum and broader EICT reach in Northeast India.

IIT Roorkee Data Science — Strengths

  • India's oldest IIT (established 1847) — legacy brand recognition
  • NIRF #7 Engineering — consistent top-10 ranking
  • Strong employer recognition in manufacturing, BFSI, and consulting sectors
  • IIT Roorkee alumni network in North India particularly dense
  • Curriculum strength in ML/DL and applied AI for industry

IIT Guwahati Data Science — Strengths

  • ₹2.1L vs ₹2.5L — meaningful saving for similar IIT calibre
  • EICT (Education & Research in Information and Communication Technology) delivery — India-wide reach
  • NIRF #8 Engineering — strong technical reputation
  • EICT programmes have strong placement assistance infrastructure
  • IIT Guwahati alumni network strong in Northeast, East India, and government/PSU sectors

How to Choose

Choose IIT Roorkee if: you are in North India / Delhi-NCR / Pune / Mumbai where IIT Roorkee brand is well-recognised by employers; you have ₹2.5L budget; your target employers are large tech companies, banks or consulting firms where IIT Roorkee carries known brand weight.

Choose IIT Guwahati if: ₹2.1L budget is the constraint; you are in Northeast, East or Central India where IIT Guwahati alumni network is stronger; or you prefer EICT's broader reach and placement assistance model.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIT Roorkee or IIT Guwahati better for data science?

Both are top-10 IITs with strong data science programmes. IIT Roorkee (NIRF #7) has higher brand recognition nationally; IIT Guwahati (NIRF #8, EICT) has broader regional reach and is ₹40K cheaper. For most working professionals in metro cities, IIT Roorkee has slightly higher employer recognition.

What is the EICT Academy?

EICT (Education & Research in Information and Communication Technology) Academy is a joint initiative by IIT Guwahati and other IITs to deliver technology education programmes across India. EICT programmes carry the institutional credibility of IIT Guwahati and are delivered through a network of centres across the country.

What is the salary after IIT data science certificate?

Data Scientists with IIT certificate: ₹8-15L (0-3 yrs post-cert). Senior Data Scientist: ₹15-25L (3-6 yrs). Data Science Lead/Manager with IIT background: ₹22-40L (6+ yrs). The IIT credential is most impactful in the first job after the programme — subsequent compensation growth depends more on project experience and domain expertise.

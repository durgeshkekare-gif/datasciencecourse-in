IIT Roorkee Applied DS & AI — What It Is

The Post Graduate Certificate Programme in Applied Data Science & AI from IIT Roorkee (via E&ICT Academy) is an 11-month programme for working professionals. Fee: approximately Rs 2,50,000 + GST. This is the technical-depth IIT credential for data science — covering Python, machine learning, deep learning, NLP, and AI deployment. IIT Roorkee is NIRF #6 overall and among India's oldest IITs.

Curriculum — What You Actually Learn

  • Python for data science — pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, scikit-learn
  • Machine learning — supervised, unsupervised, ensemble methods
  • Deep learning — CNNs, RNNs, transformers (PyTorch / Keras)
  • Natural language processing — text classification, BERT, LLMs
  • Computer vision — image classification, object detection, GANs
  • MLOps and deployment — model serving, Docker, cloud deployment
  • Capstone project — end-to-end DS/AI project on real data

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIT Roorkee Data Science certificate worth it?

Yes — IIT Roorkee is NIRF #6, and the E&ICT Academy DS & AI programme is among India's most technically rigorous data science credentials for working professionals. At Rs 2,50,000 for 11 months with IIT Roorkee alumni status, it is better value than most private data science bootcamps that cost similar fees with no institutional brand.

What is the curriculum of IIT Roorkee Applied DS & AI programme?

Curriculum covers Python, machine learning (supervised/unsupervised), deep learning (CNNs, RNNs, transformers), NLP, computer vision, and MLOps/deployment. Python basics are recommended before joining — the programme moves quickly from fundamentals to advanced topics. Capstone project on real data is part of completion.

IIT Roorkee or IIT Guwahati for Data Science — which is better?

Both are excellent. IIT Roorkee (NIRF #6 overall) has slightly stronger brand recognition nationally. IIT Guwahati (NIRF #7 overall) is comparable and typically Rs 30-40K cheaper. Curriculum is similar. Choose IIT Roorkee for slightly stronger brand; IIT Guwahati for marginally better value.
